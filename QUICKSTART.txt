FOLBUM v0.2.0

1. After pulling the release image on the NAS, open ZimaOS Custom App Install.
   Choose Import > Docker Compose and paste Apps/Folbum/docker-compose.yml
   from the matching release. Keep app-data mounted at /app/data and click Install.
2. Open http://NAS_IP:8765 on your trusted LAN, create your account, then sign in.
3. Open Connection and add your ZimaOS NAS.
4. Choose a Photos source, review its album preview, then run sync.

Use a trusted LAN or HTTPS proxy. See README.md for release limitations.
To build from a source checkout, run docker compose up -d --build on the NAS.
