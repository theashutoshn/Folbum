ZIMA ALBUM SYNC - LOCAL TEST

1. Copy .env.example to .env and set APP_PASSWORD to a unique password.
2. Run docker compose up -d --build.
3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765 and sign in as admin.
4. In demo mode, choose the sample Photos source and save it.
5. Select folders, save rules, review the preview, and run sync.

For your NAS, switch ZIMA_MODE to live and ENABLE_LIVE_WRITES to true,
restart the app, then connect to your own ZimaOS address from the UI.
Use a trusted LAN or HTTPS proxy. See README.md for release limitations.
