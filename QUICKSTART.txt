FOLBUM - PUBLIC BETA v0.2.0

1. Copy .env.example to .env.
2. Run docker compose up -d --build.
3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765, create your account, then sign in.
4. Open Connection and add your ZimaOS NAS.
5. Choose a Photos source, review its album preview, then run sync.

Use a trusted LAN or HTTPS proxy. See README.md for release limitations.
